# AI-Powered Enterprise Knowledge Assistant

## 1. Problem Statement

Employees spend significant time searching across internal documentation,
knowledge bases, policies, and other company resources to find information
needed to perform their work.

The goal is to provide an AI-powered assistant that allows employees to ask
questions in natural language and receive useful answers grounded in approved
company information.

## 2. Product Vision

Create a trusted enterprise AI assistant that helps employees find and
understand company knowledge faster while maintaining appropriate security,
privacy, and access controls.

## 3. Target Users

- Employees
- Engineering teams
- Program and project managers
- Operations teams
- Support teams
- New employees during onboarding

## 4. Example Use Cases

- Find company policies
- Search technical documentation
- Find project information
- Answer onboarding questions
- Summarize internal documentation
- Identify relevant resources for a specific task

## 5. MVP Scope

The initial MVP will:

- Accept natural-language questions
- Search a defined collection of approved documents
- Generate answers based on those documents
- Provide references to the source documents
- Clearly indicate when information cannot be found
- Respect basic document access permissions

## 6. Out of Scope for MVP

- Autonomous decision making
- Making changes to company systems
- Accessing confidential systems without authorization
- Replacing human experts
- Fully autonomous AI agents

## 7. Success Metrics

Initial success will be measured through:

- Answer accuracy
- Percentage of answers supported by source documents
- User satisfaction
- Time saved finding information
- Percentage of questions successfully answered
- Hallucination/error rate

## 8. Key Program Risks

- AI hallucinations
- Incorrect or outdated source information
- Data privacy
- Security/access control
- Poor answer quality
- User trust
- Performance/latency
- AI infrastructure cost

## 9. Key Stakeholders

- Product Management
- Engineering
- AI/ML Engineering
- Data Engineering
- Security
- Privacy/Legal
- IT
- Business Operations
- End Users

## 10. Initial Program Goal

Build and evaluate a working prototype that demonstrates the value of
AI-assisted enterprise knowledge retrieval while identifying the technical,
security, privacy, and operational requirements necessary for production.
